Welcome

A church family at the heart of Milngavie

Our congregation is situated in Milngavie (pronounced 'Mil-guy'), a suburb to the north-west of Glasgow, and we are linked with Baldernock church. Together we love, serve and worship the Lord Jesus Christ.
